About

I’m a software engineer based in Bengaluru, currently completing my degree in Computer Science with a specialization in Artificial Intelligence at Sir M. Visvesvaraya Institute of Technology, alongside an AI and Data Science minor program at IIT Mandi.

My strongest engineering direction centers on backend systems, hybrid retrieval architecture, and applied AI. Rather than treating machine learning as an isolated API call or an abstract demo, I care about the engineering discipline required to make systems reliable in production — transaction boundaries, asynchronous pipelines, indexing performance, and verifiable correctness.

Before focusing deeply on systems engineering, I worked across freelance visual design, UI/UX, and web development. Having that design foundation keeps me grounded in the complete product: understanding the problem, designing the system, and ensuring deep backend architectures surface into interfaces that feel intuitive, fast, and thoughtful for real people.
